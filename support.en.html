Frequently Asked Questions

1. Where do I find my Issuer ID, Key ID, Vendor Number, and .p8 file?

Sign in to App Store Connect on the web:

  • Go to Users and Access → Integrations → App Store Connect API, create a key with Sales and Reports or Finance access, copy your Issuer ID and Key ID, and download the AuthKey_XXXX.p8 file;
  • Go to Sales and Trends or Payments and Financial Reports to find your 8-digit Vendor Number (usually starting with 8 or 9) in the top-left corner.

2. Why does "All Time" show slightly more downloads than the 90-day view on ASC Web?

App Store Connect's web Analytics defaults to a rolling 90-day window, whereas ASC Watcher's "All Time" tab aggregates up to 12 months of historical MONTHLY reports plus recent DAILY reports. Select a matching date range filter to compare the exact same window.

3. Why are at most two original currencies shown in parentheses?

To keep cards clean when an app earns revenue across dozens of currencies, ASC Watcher sorts original settlement currencies by converted value and displays the top 2 original currencies (e.g., ($6.79 + ₹575.55)) underneath the converted total.

4. How do I add the Home Screen Widget?

Open More → Home Screen Widget and tap Pin Widget to Home Screen, or long-press an empty space on your phone's home screen, open the system Widget gallery, and drag the ASC Watcher (4×2) widget onto your home screen.
