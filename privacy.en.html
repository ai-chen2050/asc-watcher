Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-10-30 · Applies to ASC Watcher for iOS & Android

ASC Watcher is a local-first App Store Connect sales and download analytics utility. We have no user account system and operate zero middleman or backend servers. Below is a clear breakdown of what is stored, where it lives, what direct network calls occur, and how to delete your data.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information or financial data. There is no sign-up or login, no device fingerprinting, no location or contact access, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. Your App Store Connect API credentials (Issuer ID, Key ID, Vendor Number, and .p8 private key) and all downloaded sales and download reports remain 100% on your device. We cannot view or access them.

2. Where Your Data Is Stored

All credentials and cached TSV report data are stored locally within the app's private sandbox storage on your device. Summary figures displayed on the Home Screen Widget are likewise stored in local shared preferences on your phone and never leave the device.

3. Direct Network Requests & Third-Party Services

No traffic ever passes through any server owned by us. Your device makes direct HTTPS requests only to:

  • Apple App Store Connect API (api.appstoreconnect.apple.com): Using ES256 JWT tokens signed locally on your device to fetch your registered app list and daily/monthly sales TSV reports directly from Apple;
  • Apple iTunes Lookup API (itunes.apple.com): To resolve public high-resolution app icons by Apple ID or Bundle ID;
  • Public Exchange Rate Endpoint: To fetch reference currency conversion rates (USD, CNY, EUR, GBP, JPY, etc.) with zero user identifiers attached.

4. Optional Bring-Your-Own-Key Extensions

In builds that support optional custom API keys for external analysis, requests are sent directly from your device to the provider you configure without passing through any server of ours.

6. Data Deletion & Disconnecting

You can clear cached TSV reports anytime under "More → Clear Report Cache", or wipe your saved Issuer ID, Key ID, Vendor Number, and .p8 private key under "More → Disconnect Account". Uninstalling the app removes all local data from your device.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
