# ASC Watcher (asc-watcher, ASC Watcher App, ASC 开发者看板, App Store Connect 销量监控)

> ASC Watcher is a privacy-first, local-only App Store Connect sales, review, and release companion for Apple developers. Connecting directly to Apple's official API via on-device ES256 JWT signing, it aggregates daily and monthly TSV reports for net proceeds, calibrated first-time downloads, IAP breakdowns, 9-currency conversions with top-2 original currencies, in-app customer review replies, App Store review & TestFlight monitoring with 1-tap release, and a 4×2 Home Screen Widget. Zero middleman servers—your .p8 key never leaves your phone.

## Overview

- **Name**: ASC Watcher
- **Also known as**: asc-watcher, ASC Watcher App, ASC 开发者看板, App Store Connect 销量监控
- **Positioning**: 100% local-first App Store Connect sales, first-time downloads, review replies & release companion
- **Platforms**: iOS / Android
- **App Store**: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6828810863

## Core Features

1. **On-device ES256 JWT direct to Apple API**: Your Issuer ID, Key ID, Vendor Number, and .p8 private key never leave your phone. JWT tokens are signed locally to query api.appstoreconnect.apple.com directly.
2. **Calibrated first-time downloads & IAP metrics**: Strictly separates first-time downloads (1/1F/1T/F1) from In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (IA1/IA9/IAY) while excluding auto-updates (7/7F/7T) and redownloads.
3. **Interactive bar chart drill-downs & 9-currency conversion**: Tap any bar to inspect the exact apps, IAPs, or countries for that day; switch between 9 target currencies while viewing the top 2 original settlement currencies side by side.
4. **Global customer reviews & direct in-app replies**: Connects directly to /v1/apps/{id}/customerReviews and /v1/customerReviewResponses so you can filter reviews by star rating and post, edit, or delete official developer replies on the go.
5. **App review status, TestFlight builds & 1-tap release**: Track App Store version states (In Review, Waiting for Review, Rejected, Pending Developer Release, Ready for Sale) and TestFlight builds, with 1-tap manual release right from your phone.
6. **4×2 Live Home Screen Widget**: Pin a 4×2 dashboard widget to your iOS or Android home screen to check net developer proceeds, yesterday's downloads, total first-time downloads, and paid transactions at a glance.
