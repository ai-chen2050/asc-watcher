# ASC Watcher (asc-watcher, ASC Watcher App, ASC 开发者看板, App Store Connect 销量监控)

> ASC Watcher is a privacy-first, local-only App Store Connect sales and download analytics companion for indie developers and teams. Connecting directly to Apple's official API via on-device ES256 JWT signing, it aggregates daily and monthly TSV reports to track net developer proceeds, first-time downloads, in-app purchases, geographic breakdowns, and multi-currency conversions. With zero middleman servers and zero analytics SDKs, your .p8 private key and financial records never leave your device, complete with a live Home Screen Widget.

## Overview

- **Name**: ASC Watcher
- **Also known as**: asc-watcher, ASC Watcher App, ASC 开发者看板, App Store Connect 销量监控
- **Positioning**: 100% local-first App Store Connect sales, first-time downloads & IAP analytics dashboard
- **Platforms**: iOS / Android

## Core Features

1. **On-device ES256 JWT direct to Apple API**: Your Issuer ID, Key ID, Vendor Number, and .p8 private key never leave your phone. JWT tokens are signed locally to query api.appstoreconnect.apple.com directly.
2. **Calibrated first-time downloads & IAP metrics**: Strictly separates first-time downloads (1/1F/1T/F1) from In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (IA1/IA9/IAY) while excluding auto-updates (7/7F/7T) and redownloads.
3. **Interactive bar charts with daily breakdown**: Switch between net proceeds, first-time downloads, and IAP charts; tap any bar to lock it and inspect the exact apps, products, or countries that make up that day's total.
4. **Multi-currency auto conversion & top-2 original currencies**: Switch seamlessly between 9 display currencies (USD, CNY, EUR, GBP, JPY, HKD, TWD, AUD, CAD) while viewing the top 2 original settlement currencies side by side.
5. **Live Home Screen Widget**: Pin a 4×2 dashboard widget to your phone's home screen to check net developer proceeds, yesterday's downloads, total first-time downloads, and paid transactions at a glance.
