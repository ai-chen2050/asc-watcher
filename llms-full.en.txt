## 3. Features in detail

### 3.1 On-device ES256 JWT direct to Apple API
- **Summary**: Your Issuer ID, Key ID, Vendor Number, and .p8 private key never leave your phone. JWT tokens are signed locally to query api.appstoreconnect.apple.com directly.
- **Detail**: Many third-party analytics tools require uploading your App Store Connect private key to their cloud servers. ASC Watcher eliminates middleman risk: every request goes straight from your device to Apple.

### 3.2 Calibrated first-time downloads & IAP metrics
- **Summary**: Strictly separates first-time downloads (1/1F/1T/F1) from In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (IA1/IA9/IAY) while excluding auto-updates (7/7F/7T) and redownloads.
- **Detail**: Automatically merges historical monthly reports with recent daily reports without overlap, fetches all registered apps via /v1/apps, and resolves HD app icons.

### 3.3 Interactive bar chart drill-downs & 9-currency conversion
- **Summary**: Tap any bar to inspect the exact apps, IAPs, or countries for that day; switch between 9 target currencies while viewing the top 2 original settlement currencies side by side.
- **Detail**: Free download rows never pollute currency buckets—only rows with non-zero Developer Proceeds are aggregated into original settlement currencies.

### 3.4 Global customer reviews & direct in-app replies
- **Summary**: Connects directly to /v1/apps/{id}/customerReviews and /v1/customerReviewResponses so you can filter reviews by star rating and post, edit, or delete official developer replies on the go.
- **Detail**: Includes 1-tap quick reply templates and keeps the App Detail view clean by showing the latest review by default with 1-tap expansion.

### 3.5 App review status, TestFlight builds & 1-tap release
- **Summary**: Track App Store version states (In Review, Waiting for Review, Rejected, Pending Developer Release, Ready for Sale) and TestFlight builds, with 1-tap manual release right from your phone.
- **Detail**: Triggers official POST /v1/appStoreVersionReleaseRequests directly from your device so you can release approved versions anywhere.

### 3.6 4×2 Live Home Screen Widget
- **Summary**: Pin a 4×2 dashboard widget to your iOS or Android home screen to check net developer proceeds, yesterday's downloads, total first-time downloads, and paid transactions at a glance.
- **Detail**: Adapts to Light and Dark themes with an in-app live preview and one-tap home screen pinning.

## 4. FAQ

### Does ASC Watcher upload my .p8 private key or sales data to your servers?
Never. ASC Watcher has no user accounts and zero backend servers. Your Issuer ID, Key ID, Vendor Number, and .p8 private key are stored exclusively on your device. All ES256 JWT tokens are signed locally and sent directly to Apple's official endpoint (api.appstoreconnect.apple.com).

### Why can I see data before configuring my API key?
When no credentials are configured, the app starts in built-in Demo Mode so you can explore interactive charts, currency switching, review replies, and version release workflows right away. Add your real API key anytime under More → API Credentials.

### What role should I assign to my App Store Connect API Key?
For sales and download reports only, a read-only Finance or Sales and Reports role is sufficient. To reply to customer reviews or trigger 1-tap version releases from the app, assign Customer Support, App Manager, or Admin.

### Can I reply to customer reviews and release approved versions directly in the app?
Yes. In any App Detail page, the latest version status and latest customer review are shown cleanly by default with 1-tap expansion. You can post, edit, or delete official developer responses using quick templates, and release versions in PENDING_DEVELOPER_RELEASE state with a single tap.

### How does pricing work? Can I still use the app after 100 opens?
Yes, you can keep using it. Your first 100 app opens are 100% silent with zero paywall prompts and all features unlocked. After 100 opens, the app gently shows a dismissible Pro upgrade sheet on launch (with a 2-hour cooldown) that you can close anytime to continue using the app, or upgrade to Pro (¥6/mo, ¥38/yr, or ¥68 lifetime) to permanently remove launch prompts.

### How do I add the Home Screen Widget?
Go to More → Home Screen Widget to preview the widget in Light/Dark mode and tap 'Pin to Home Screen', or long-press an empty area on your phone's home screen and add the ASC Watcher (4×2) widget from the system widget gallery.
