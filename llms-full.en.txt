## 3. Features in detail

### 3.1 On-device ES256 JWT direct to Apple API
- **Summary**: Your Issuer ID, Key ID, Vendor Number, and .p8 private key never leave your phone. JWT tokens are signed locally to query api.appstoreconnect.apple.com directly.
- **Detail**: Many third-party analytics tools require uploading your App Store Connect private key to their cloud servers. ASC Watcher eliminates middleman risk: every request goes straight from your device to Apple.

### 3.2 Calibrated first-time downloads & IAP metrics
- **Summary**: Strictly separates first-time downloads (1/1F/1T/F1) from In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (IA1/IA9/IAY) while excluding auto-updates (7/7F/7T) and redownloads.
- **Detail**: Automatically merges historical monthly reports with recent daily reports without overlap, fetches all registered apps via /v1/apps, and resolves HD app icons.

### 3.3 Interactive bar charts with daily breakdown
- **Summary**: Switch between net proceeds, first-time downloads, and IAP charts; tap any bar to lock it and inspect the exact apps, products, or countries that make up that day's total.
- **Detail**: Across the main Sales dashboard, Trends tab, and individual App Detail pages, tap metric cards to switch charts and expand full 90-day daily histories.

### 3.4 Multi-currency auto conversion & top-2 original currencies
- **Summary**: Switch seamlessly between 9 display currencies (USD, CNY, EUR, GBP, JPY, HKD, TWD, AUD, CAD) while viewing the top 2 original settlement currencies side by side.
- **Detail**: Free download rows never pollute currency buckets—only rows with non-zero Developer Proceeds are aggregated into original settlement currencies.

### 3.5 Live Home Screen Widget
- **Summary**: Pin a 4×2 dashboard widget to your phone's home screen to check net developer proceeds, yesterday's downloads, total first-time downloads, and paid transactions at a glance.
- **Detail**: Adapts to Light and Dark themes with an in-app live preview and one-tap home screen pinning.

## 4. FAQ

### Does ASC Watcher upload my .p8 private key or sales data to your servers?
Never. ASC Watcher has no user accounts and no backend servers of its own. Your Issuer ID, Key ID, Vendor Number, and .p8 private key are stored strictly on your device, and ES256 JWT tokens are signed locally to communicate directly with Apple (api.appstoreconnect.apple.com).

### Why can I see data before configuring an API key?
When launched without credentials, ASC Watcher provides a built-in Demo Mode with realistic sample data so you can explore every chart, currency switch, and breakdown immediately. Configure your real key anytime under More → Credentials Setup.

### What role should I assign to my App Store Connect API Key?
We recommend creating a key under App Store Connect → Users and Access → Integrations with read-only 'Sales and Reports' or 'Finance' access, following the principle of least privilege.

### How do I add the Home Screen Widget?
Go to More → Home Screen Widget to preview the widget and tap 'Pin Widget to Home Screen', or long-press an empty area on your phone's home screen and select ASC Watcher from the system widget gallery.
